## 2. Core Rules

1. Matrix adalah planning layer, bukan NPC registry.
2. Tidak ada NPC individual yang dibuat oleh dokumen ini.
3. Satu NPC boleh memenuhi beberapa scope hanya jika hubungan scope tersebut benar-benar material secara lore.
4. NPC tidak boleh dihitung ke scope yang lebih tinggi hanya karena lokasi geografisnya berada di dalam scope tersebut.
5. Dynamic NPC dan Persistent Dynamic NPC tidak dihitung sebagai Canon NPC coverage kecuali Admin melakukan Canonization terpisah.
6. Tidak boleh membuat faction Canon baru hanya untuk memenuhi coverage.
7. Membership, rank, authority, access, reputation, resources, knowledge, dan loyalty tidak diwariskan otomatis dari faction atau lokasi.

## 8. Faction Assignment

Faction assignment must follow actual NPC function. A faction reference in this Matrix is context only and does not create membership.

If no Canon faction is materially appropriate, use `???` or an allowed Dynamic Faction. Never create a Canon faction merely to fill a slot.

## 9. Role Integrity

Roles must be justified by geography, population, governance, economy, faction, or other established Canon context.

Suitable role bands include governance, administration, security, military, trade, agriculture, mining, transport, logistics, maritime, shipbuilding, forestry, craft, pastoral, caravan, frontier, services, specialist, and community roles when supported by context.

Religious, noble-house, academic, criminal, adventuring, or other institutional roles remain `???` until a valid Canon context exists.

## 10. Overlap Policy

### Allowed

- Empire official with genuine cross-kingdom responsibility.
- Kingdom official with genuine city responsibility.
- City/settlement figure with documented regional influence.
- Faction leader whose actual duties span multiple scopes.
- Specialist with legitimate cross-territorial function.

### Forbidden

- Reusing one NPC across scopes solely to reach quota.
- Assigning artificial authority to expand coverage.
- Counting location containment as automatic scope relevance.

## 11. Anti-Filler Gate

Reject candidate if it exists only because of quota, lacks material function/agency, repeats another NPC without reason, uses faction only as a slot-filler, has unsupported location, has template-only background, has undefined knowledge boundary, lacks traceable Origin, or uses a non-Canon Race.

## 12. Anti-Duplicate Gate

Before creation:

```text
CANON NPC REGISTRY CHECK
↓
EXISTING CANON NPC RECORD CHECK
↓
PERSISTENT DYNAMIC NPC CHECK
↓
NAME / ROLE / LOCATION / FACTION COLLISION CHECK
↓
RELATIONSHIP / BACKGROUND COLLISION CHECK
↓
CANONIZATION
```

Similarity alone is not duplicate; identity and lore determine the result.

## 13. Race Safety

Every Canon NPC must use an active `RACE_CANON_ID` from `races/CANON_REGISTRY.md`.

Race must never be inferred from name, location, role, faction, appearance stereotype, or profession. Race does not automatically determine personality, morality, class, faction, loyalty, capability, or outcome.
