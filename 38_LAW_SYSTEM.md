# ELDORIA WORLD — LAW & SUCCESSION SYSTEM

> **Module:** 38 — Law & Succession System
> **Canon:** ELDORIA CANON v1.0 — LOCKED
> **Status:** Admin Canon v1.0
> **Phase:** WORLD DEVELOPMENT

## 1. Purpose

Menetapkan legal authority, hierarchy of law, succession, title recognition, dan batas kewenangan Empire serta lima Kingdom.

Module ini menjadi dasar hukum untuk `37_NOBILITY_SYSTEM.md`, governance, title, domain, inheritance, military obligation, taxation, dan political resolution.

## 2. Hierarchy of Law

```text
IMPERIAL LAW
    ↓
KINGDOM LAW
    ↓
REGIONAL / LOCAL REGULATION
    ↓
FACTION / CONTRACTUAL RULES
```

Lower-level rules tidak boleh bertentangan dengan higher-level law yang berlaku.

Contract atau faction rule hanya mengikat dalam scope yang sah dan tidak menghapus kewajiban hukum publik.

## 3. Imperial Legal Baseline

Kekaisaran Valthera mempertahankan:

- common imperial sovereignty;
- common defense obligations;
- recognition of Kingdom governments;
- inter-kingdom legal coordination;
- imperial authority atas perkara yang secara eksplisit berada dalam Imperial scope.

Kingdom mempertahankan internal autonomy untuk administration, local justice, taxation, land administration, military organization, dan succession selama tidak bertentangan dengan Imperial law.

Empire tidak otomatis mengelola seluruh urusan lokal Kingdom.

## 4. Imperial Succession Recognition

Tahta Emperor/Empress mengikuti hereditary succession yang ditetapkan oleh Imperial succession law.

Untuk gameplay resolution, succession harus memeriksa:

```text
VALID CLAIM
→ ELIGIBILITY
→ SUCCESSION ORDER
→ LEGAL RECOGNITION
→ CURRENT STATE
```

Kematian atau hilangnya seorang ruler tidak otomatis menciptakan holder baru tanpa resolution.

Imperial authority dapat mengakui atau menolak legal succession hanya berdasarkan kewenangan hukum yang relevan; tidak boleh mengganti claimant secara arbitrer.

## 5. Kingdom Succession — Universal Rule

Setiap Kingdom menggunakan hereditary succession dengan **designated eligible heir** sebagai prinsip utama.

Urutan umum:

1. designated heir yang memenuhi legal eligibility;
2. eligible direct descendants menurut local succession preference;
3. eligible collateral dynastic claimant bila jalur direct descendant tidak tersedia;
4. lawful succession resolution bila seluruh jalur normal gagal atau diperselisihkan.

Jenis kelamin tidak menjadi automatic disqualification.

House membership, title, wealth, military strength, magical ability, atau popularity tidak otomatis menentukan succession.

## 6. KINGDOM-001 — VALEDORN

### Government Authority

```text
HEAD_OF_STATE: King/Queen
CENTRAL_BODY: Royal Court of Valedorn
EXECUTIVE_ADMINISTRATION: Crown-appointed stewards and royal offices
LOCAL_ADMINISTRATION: Regional, city, and settlement authorities under Crown law
```

Crown memegang royal executive authority, title grants, high-level land/domain grants, foreign/inter-kingdom representation, dan kingdom defense dalam batas Imperial law.

Regional/local authorities menangani administration yang didelegasikan dan tidak memperoleh sovereignty dari jabatan.

### Succession

Valedorn memakai **designated-heir primogeniture** dengan gender-neutral eligibility.

Jika designated heir tidak eligible atau tidak dapat menerima tahta, succession berpindah ke eligible direct descendant berikutnya menurut legal order.

Jika jalur direct descendant habis, eligible collateral claimant dapat dipertimbangkan melalui lawful succession resolution.

### Noble Authority

Noble authority terutama berasal dari royal grant, recognized title, lawful domain administration, dan delegated office.

## 7. KINGDOM-002 — BRANNOR

### Government Authority

```text
HEAD_OF_STATE: King/Queen
CENTRAL_BODY: High Crown Council of Brannor
EXECUTIVE_ADMINISTRATION: Crown officers and domain stewards
LOCAL_ADMINISTRATION: Highland, city, and settlement authorities under Crown law
```

High Crown Council berfungsi sebagai institutional confirmation body untuk major succession disputes, major domain transfers, dan kingdom-wide matters yang ditentukan hukum.

Council tidak menggantikan sovereign sebagai head of state.

### Succession

Brannor memakai **designated-heir primogeniture** dengan gender-neutral eligibility.

Designated heir harus memenuhi legal eligibility dan dapat menjalankan obligations of the Crown.

Jika succession diperselisihkan, High Crown Council melakukan legal confirmation; council tidak dapat menciptakan claimant yang tidak memiliki lawful basis.

### Noble Authority

Noble authority kuat pada domain, mountain routes, resource administration, dan frontier obligations, tetapi tetap bergantung pada lawful grant dan delegated authority.

## 8. KINGDOM-003 — MARISELLE

### Government Authority

```text
HEAD_OF_STATE: Queen/King
CENTRAL_BODY: Maritime Crown Council of Mariselle
EXECUTIVE_ADMINISTRATION: Crown offices and port authorities
LOCAL_ADMINISTRATION: Port, island, coastal, and settlement authorities under Crown law
```

Crown mengatur kingdom-wide diplomacy, major ports, maritime defense, royal appointments, dan inter-kingdom trade authority sesuai hukum.

Port authorities hanya memiliki authority yang didelegasikan dan tidak otomatis memiliki military sovereignty.

### Succession

Mariselle memakai **designated-heir primogeniture** dengan gender-neutral eligibility.

Maritime Crown Council memberikan legal confirmation pada disputed succession dan memastikan continuity of Crown obligations.

Council tidak dapat mengubah succession order tanpa legal basis.

### Noble Authority

Authority bangsawan dapat terkait dengan lawful coastal/island domain, port administration, maritime obligations, dan royal delegation.

## 9. KINGDOM-004 — SYLVARAN

### Government Authority

```text
HEAD_OF_STATE: King/Queen
CENTRAL_BODY: Royal Stewardship Council of Sylvaran
EXECUTIVE_ADMINISTRATION: Crown stewards and regional wardens
LOCAL_ADMINISTRATION: Forest, river, city, and settlement authorities under Crown law
```

Royal Stewardship Council memberi institutional oversight atas major land/resource stewardship dan disputed succession.

Council tidak memiliki sovereignty terpisah dari Crown.

### Succession

Sylvaran memakai **designated-heir primogeniture** dengan gender-neutral eligibility.

Succession resolution wajib mempertimbangkan legal eligibility dan continuity of Crown stewardship obligations.

Jika direct line tidak tersedia, collateral claimant harus memiliki lawful dynastic basis sebelum dapat dipertimbangkan.

### Noble Authority

Authority bangsawan terutama dapat berasal dari lawful stewardship, domain grant, frontier delegation, dan royal office.

## 10. KINGDOM-005 — SAHRAD

### Government Authority

```text
HEAD_OF_STATE: King/Queen
CENTRAL_BODY: Royal Oasis Council of Sahrad
EXECUTIVE_ADMINISTRATION: Crown officials and route/oasis authorities
LOCAL_ADMINISTRATION: Oasis, caravan-route, city, and settlement authorities under Crown law
```

Royal Oasis Council memberi institutional confirmation untuk major succession disputes dan kingdom-wide water/route matters yang diberikan hukum.

Council tidak memiliki sovereignty independen.

### Succession

Sahrad memakai **designated-heir primogeniture** dengan gender-neutral eligibility.

Legal succession mempertimbangkan eligibility, designated heir status, dan continuity of Crown obligations terhadap water access, caravan routes, dan frontier security.

Jika direct line tidak tersedia, collateral claimant memerlukan lawful dynastic basis.

### Noble Authority

Authority bangsawan atas oasis, route, pastoral land, atau water infrastructure hanya berlaku jika terdapat lawful grant, recognized title, administration right, atau delegated authority.

## 11. Title Grant & Recognition

```text
EMPEROR/EMPRESS → Imperial titles and imperial grants within law
KING/QUEEN      → Kingdom titles and kingdom grants within law
DELEGATED AUTHORITY → only within granted scope
```

Title tidak otomatis memberi:

- sovereignty;
- military command;
- tax monopoly;
- resource monopoly;
- magical authority;
- judicial supremacy.

Semua harus memiliki legal basis yang sesuai.

## 12. Domain Authority

Domain status harus membedakan:

```text
LEGAL OWNERSHIP
LEGAL CONTROL
ADMINISTRATION
CLAIM
INFLUENCE
OCCUPATION
```

A noble dapat mengadministrasikan domain tanpa menjadi sovereign owner.

Land, water, mine, port, forest, road, dan other resource rights harus memiliki basis legal atau contractual yang sah.

## 13. Military Authority

Military command berasal dari:

```text
CROWN / IMPERIAL AUTHORITY
→ lawful military office
→ delegated command
→ lawful local defense authority
```

Noble title saja tidak menciptakan military command.

## 14. Tax & Revenue Authority

Taxation wajib memiliki legal authority.

Noble House tidak boleh mengenakan pajak hanya karena memiliki title atau influence.

Royal/local authorities dapat mendelegasikan collection rights, tetapi delegation harus memiliki scope, duration, dan legal basis.

## 15. Disputed Succession

Jika succession diperselisihkan:

```text
CLAIMANTS IDENTIFIED
↓
ELIGIBILITY CHECK
↓
SUCCESSION ORDER CHECK
↓
LEGAL / INSTITUTIONAL CONFIRMATION
↓
STATE UPDATE
↓
HISTORY + ORIGIN
```

Tidak ada automatic winner berdasarkan power, popularity, military force, wealth, race, atau magic.

Jika legal authority tidak cukup untuk menyelesaikan dispute, outcome tetap unresolved sampai lawful resolution tersedia.

## 16. Law vs Faction

Faction dapat mempengaruhi politics tetapi tidak menciptakan law secara otomatis.

```text
FACTION INFLUENCE ≠ LEGAL AUTHORITY
HOUSE POWER ≠ SOVEREIGNTY
NPC POSITION ≠ UNLIMITED AUTHORITY
```

## 17. Runtime Safety

Untuk action yang bergantung pada hukum:

1. Load `38_LAW_SYSTEM.md`.
2. Load `37_NOBILITY_SYSTEM.md` bila title/House/domain relevan.
3. Load Governance dan relevant faction/NPC/state.
4. Verify authority dan eligibility.
5. Resolve.
6. Validate.
7. Persist material changes with History + Origin.

Jika legal basis tidak tersedia, jangan mengarang hukum pengganti.
